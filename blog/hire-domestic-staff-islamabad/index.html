Hire Domestic Staff Islamabad

20 November 2026 · 8 min read · ISB Domestic Staff Team

If you are ready to hire domestic staff in Islamabad, you do not need another general overview. You need tools you can use today. This article is a practical hiring kit: a job description template you can fill in, interview questions for each role, a structured trial day, an outline for your written agreement and a first-day checklist. Whether you are hiring a maid in G-11, a cook in DHA or a driver in F-7, these templates help you hire with confidence and avoid the most common mistakes.

Step 1: Write a Clear Job Description

A written job description helps you, the agency and the candidate understand exactly what the role involves. Copy and complete this template before you start searching:

Rolee.g. Full-time maid, family cook, driver, nanny, patient attendant

HouseholdNumber of adults, children (ages), elderly members, pets
Home sizeBedrooms, bathrooms, lawn, floors
Main dutiesList the five or six most important tasks
Not includedTasks the worker will not be expected to do

Live-in or live-outIf live-in, describe the room and facilities

Share this with your agency. It shortens the search and helps them send candidates who truly fit.

Keep the completed template saved on your phone. If you ever need a replacement or temporary cover, you can share it again in seconds, and anyone helping you, whether a relative or the agency, will understand your needs immediately without a long conversation.

Step 2: Choose How You Will Find Candidates

Referrals can work when the person recommending has employed the worker directly for a long time. Brokers are fast but rarely verify identity or references properly. A professional agency adds verification, matching, written terms and a replacement guarantee, which is why most families now prefer it for anyone who will live in or care for children and elders. Step 3: Screen Before You Interview

Before inviting anyone home, make sure basic checks are complete:

  • Original CNIC seen and a copy taken
  • Current and permanent addresses confirmed
  • At least one previous employer contacted directly
  • Police verification arranged for drivers, watchmen and live-in staff where needed
  • Age confirmed as an adult

A good agency completes these steps for you. If you are hiring privately, do not skip them.

Step 4: Interview With Role-Specific Questions

Start every interview by explaining the job clearly, then use general and role-specific questions.

General questions for every role

  1. Tell me about your last job. What did a normal day look like?
  2. How long did you work there, and why did you leave?
  3. May I speak to your previous employer?
  4. Where do you live, and how will you travel here each day? (for live-out roles)
  5. Is there any part of this job you are not comfortable doing?
  6. What do you expect from an employer?

For maids and housekeepers

  • How do you clean marble floors and bathrooms?
  • How would you organise weekly tasks in a four-bedroom house?
  • Have you used a washing machine and vacuum cleaner before?

For cooks

  • Which dishes do you cook best? Can you make them with less oil?
  • Have you cooked for diabetic or heart patients?
  • How do you plan meals and grocery lists for a week?
  • Can you bake or prepare continental food?

For drivers

  • Which vehicles have you driven, and do you have a valid licence?
  • Which routes do you know well in Islamabad and Rawalpindi?
  • What basic car checks do you do each morning?
  • Are you comfortable with long trips to Murree or Lahore?

For nannies and babysitters

  • What ages of children have you cared for?
  • What would you do if a child had a high fever while we were out?
  • How do you handle a tantrum?
  • What activities do you do with children indoors?

For patient attendants

  • Have you cared for bedridden patients? How do you prevent bed sores?
  • How do you move a patient from bed to wheelchair safely?
  • Are you comfortable with night duty?
  • How do you keep track of medicine times?

For watchmen

  • How do you handle a visitor you do not recognise?
  • What do you do during a power cut at night?
  • Have you kept visitor logs before?
Interview tip: Listen for specific, honest answers. "I have cleaned many houses" is less convincing than "In my last job I cleaned a five-bedroom house in F-8 with three bathrooms, every day except Friday."

Bonus: A Reference Call Script

Reference checks are only useful if you ask the right questions. When you call a previous employer, introduce yourself, explain that the candidate has applied to work with you, and use a short script like this:

  1. "How long did [name] work for you, and in what role?"
  2. "What were their main duties on a normal day?"
  3. "How would you describe their honesty and reliability?"
  4. "Were they usually on time? Did they take many unplanned days off?"
  5. "How did they get along with your children or elderly family members?"
  6. "Why did they leave?"
  7. "Would you hire them again?"

Pay attention to hesitation and to how specific the answers are. A genuine former employer can usually describe duties, timings and the family situation in detail. If the person on the phone seems to know very little, or the number belongs to a relative, treat the reference with caution and ask the candidate for another one.

When you hire through a verified agency, the agency should already have completed these calls. It is still reasonable to ask which employers were contacted and what they said.

Step 5: Run a Structured Trial Day

A trial day reveals more than any interview. Plan it carefully:

  1. Agree on payment for the trial day in advance and pay it at the end, whatever you decide.
  2. Choose real tasks that reflect the job: cleaning two rooms and a bathroom, cooking a lunch, driving a school route, or caring for a child for a few hours with a parent nearby.
  3. Observe without hovering. Notice punctuality, care, hygiene, attitude and how the person handles instructions.
  4. Ask for feedback. At the end, ask the candidate how they found the work and whether they have questions.
  5. Decide promptly. Good candidates often have other offers.

Step 6: Make an Offer and Agree Terms in Writing

Once you have chosen someone, put the arrangement in writing. Your agreement should include:

  • Names and CNIC numbers of employer and worker
  • Job title and main duties
  • Working hours, breaks and weekly off
  • Monthly salary, payment date and method
  • Annual leave and Eid arrangements
  • Accommodation and meals for live-in staff
  • Trial period length
  • Notice period on both sides
  • Agency details and replacement terms, if hired through an agency
  • Signatures and date

Give the worker a copy and keep one yourself, together with a CNIC copy, photograph and emergency contact. For more on fair terms and fees, read our guide to choosing a home staff agency in Islamabad.

Step 7: Prepare for the First Day

Use this checklist before your new staff member arrives:

  • Valuables and important documents locked away
  • Cleaning supplies, uniforms and tools ready
  • Live-in room clean, furnished and with working fan or heater
  • Written duty list and house rules prepared
  • Emergency contacts posted in the kitchen
  • Society security or watchman informed of the new worker
  • A family member available at home for most of the first day

Step 8: Review After 30 Days

After a month, sit down for a short, friendly review:

  • What is going well?
  • What needs to change in duties or timings?
  • Is the worker comfortable with the arrangement?
  • Are there any concerns on either side?

Let your agency know how the placement is going. If something is not working, raise it while you are still within the replacement guarantee period.

Common Mistakes When You Hire Domestic Staff in Islamabad

  1. Hiring in a rush without checking identity or references.
  2. Describing the job vaguely, leading to disappointment on both sides.
  3. Offering a salary far below market, which attracts less experienced workers or leads to quick departures.
  4. Skipping the trial day, especially for cooks and nannies.
  5. Relying on verbal promises instead of a simple written agreement.
  6. Adding duties gradually without discussion, which causes resentment.
  7. Ignoring early warning signs instead of addressing them calmly.

How Long Does It Take to Hire?

With a verified agency, the timeline for common roles is often short:

  • Day 1: share your job description with the agency.
  • Days 2–4: receive a shortlist of verified candidates.
  • Days 3–6: interviews and a trial day.
  • Within a week: written agreement and start date for most maid, cook and driver roles.

Specialised roles such as experienced newborn nannies or night-shift patient attendants may take longer, because the right skills matter more than speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest safe way to hire domestic staff in Islamabad?

Using a verified agency is usually the fastest safe option, because candidates are already screened. Share a clear job description, interview promptly and arrange a short trial day.

Should I pay for a trial day?

Yes. Paying for the trial day is fair to the candidate and encourages them to show their real skills. Agree on the amount beforehand.

What documents should I keep after hiring?

Keep a copy of the worker's CNIC, a recent photograph, emergency contact details and your signed written agreement. Never keep the original CNIC.

How much salary should I offer?

Salaries depend on the role, experience, hours and whether the job is live-in. Ask your agency for current market rates in Islamabad for the specific role you need.

Can I hire domestic staff in Islamabad for part-time work?

Yes. Part-time maids, cooks, cleaners and gardeners are widely available for a few hours a day or selected days each week.
