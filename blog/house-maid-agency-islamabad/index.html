House Maid Agency Islamabad

22 November 2026 · 10 min read · ISB Domestic Staff Team

A house maid agency in Islamabad is meant to save you time and protect your home. A good one checks who a maid really is, matches her to your routine and stays with you after she starts. A weak one hands over a phone number and disappears. This guide looks at the agency itself: what happens behind the scenes, how to judge an agency before paying anything, the questions worth asking, and the warning signs that mean you should walk away.

What a House Maid Agency Actually Does

Many families think of an agency as a list of names. In practice, a proper house maid agency in Islamabad does several jobs that a household would otherwise have to do alone, and usually with less experience:

  • Sourcing: finding women who are actually looking for domestic work, through referrals, returning workers and community networks, rather than taking anyone who walks in.
  • Screening: confirming identity, checking addresses and contacting previous employers.
  • Skill assessment: understanding what each maid can really do, from basic cleaning to laundry, ironing, kitchen help or childcare support.
  • Matching: pairing a maid with a household whose hours, workload and expectations suit her.
  • Documentation: putting duties, salary, leave and notice period in writing.
  • After-placement support: following up, mediating misunderstandings and arranging a replacement when a placement does not work.

When you compare agencies, compare them on these functions, not on how quickly they promise to send someone.

Inside the Screening Process

Screening is the single biggest reason to use a house maid agency rather than hiring through a neighbour's cousin. It is also where agencies differ most. Here is what thorough screening usually involves.

Identity verification

The agency should see and keep a copy of the maid's original CNIC, and check that the face, name and details match. A maid who cannot or will not show her CNIC should not be placed in your home.

Address and family contact

A permanent address, whether in a Rawalpindi or Islamabad neighbourhood or in her home village, plus at least one family contact, gives accountability. Good agencies confirm these rather than simply writing them down.

Reference checks

Previous employers are the best source of truth about reliability, honesty and work quality. A serious agency calls them and asks specific questions: how long she worked there, what her duties were and why she left.

Police verification

For live-in placements and homes with children or elderly members, police verification adds another layer of security. Ask the agency how it handles this and how long it takes.

Personal interview

A face-to-face interview helps the agency judge attitude, communication and honesty about skills. Experienced staff notice when someone overstates what she can do, or seems unsure about the work.

Ask to see the file. Before a maid starts, a reliable agency should be willing to show you copies of her identity document and the verification it has done. If an agency refuses or becomes vague, treat that as a red flag.

How Good Matching Works

Even a well-screened maid can fail in the wrong house. Matching is where a house maid agency in Islamabad earns its value. Before suggesting anyone, the agency should ask you about:

  • The size of your home: a two-bedroom flat in a G-sector and a large house in DHA or Bahria Town are very different workloads.
  • The household: number of adults, children, elderly members and anyone with special care needs.
  • Pets: some maids are uncomfortable around dogs, and it is better to know before day one.
  • Working pattern: part-time, full-time day shift or live-in, and the exact hours you need.
  • Specific duties: cleaning only, or laundry, ironing, dishwashing and kitchen help as well.
  • Language and temperament: some families prefer a quieter worker, others someone more outgoing who gets on with children.
  • Location and travel: for day shifts, how far the maid lives and how she will reach you.

An agency that asks few questions and sends whoever is available is gambling with your time. One that asks detailed questions, and sometimes says "we do not have the right person yet," is usually the better choice.

Training and Orientation

Agencies vary in how much training they provide. Some focus mainly on placement; others run basic orientation covering:

  • Household hygiene and safe use of cleaning chemicals
  • Care of different floor types, furniture and fabrics
  • Using common appliances such as washing machines, vacuum cleaners and steam irons
  • Professional conduct: punctuality, privacy, phone use and respectful communication
  • What to do in an emergency, and whom to call

Ask what orientation the agency gives, but do not expect any agency to train a maid for your exact standards. Agency or Self-Hiring: An Honest Comparison

Hiring a maid yourself, through word of mouth, is common and sometimes works well. It is fair to look at both options honestly.

When self-hiring can work

  • You know the maid's previous employer personally and trust their account.
  • You only need light part-time help and are comfortable doing checks yourself.
  • You have time to interview, verify and manage the arrangement alone.

When an agency is the better choice

  • You need a live-in maid, or someone who will be alone with children or elderly parents.
  • You are new to Islamabad and have no trusted local network.
  • You work long hours and cannot spend weeks searching and verifying.
  • You want a written agreement and a replacement option if things go wrong.
  • Past self-hired arrangements ended badly or suddenly.

The real difference is accountability. With self-hiring, if a maid leaves without notice or something goes missing, you are on your own. With a reputable house maid agency in Islamabad, there is a verified file, a known office and a team whose reputation depends on resolving the problem.

Questions to Ask Before You Choose an Agency

A short phone call or office visit can tell you a lot. Ask these questions and listen to how clearly they are answered:

  1. What checks do you do on every maid? Look for CNIC, address, references and police verification where needed.
  2. Can I see the verification before she starts?
  3. Can I interview the maid before deciding? A good agency encourages this.
  4. Is there a trial period? A day or two helps both sides judge fit.
  5. What is included in your fee, and when is it paid? Get the full terms clearly, ideally in writing.
  6. What is your replacement policy? Ask how long it covers, how many replacements, and under what conditions.
  7. Do you provide a written agreement? It should cover duties, hours, salary, leave and notice.
  8. Who do I contact if there is a problem after placement? A named person and number is better than a general line.
  9. Do you have an office I can visit? A physical office signals a business that intends to stay.
  10. How do you treat the maids you place? Agencies that respect workers tend to attract better ones.

Red Flags When Choosing a House Maid Agency in Islamabad

Most problems families describe with agencies could have been spotted early. Be cautious if an agency:

  • Promises a maid within hours without asking anything about your home. Speed without screening is a risk, not a service.
  • Has no fixed office or only a mobile number, and avoids giving a location.
  • Will not show identity documents or verification for the maid.
  • Asks for full payment upfront before you have met anyone.
  • Gives vague answers about replacements, refunds or what happens if the maid leaves.
  • Has no written terms or refuses to put promises on paper.
  • Pressures the maid in front of you, or speaks about workers disrespectfully.
  • Sends someone different from the person you agreed to meet.

One red flag may have an innocent explanation. Several together usually mean you should keep looking.

What the Process Looks Like, Step by Step

Although each agency has its own approach, a well-run placement usually follows a clear sequence:

  1. First conversation: you describe your home, family, hours and duties.
  2. Shortlisting: the agency suggests one or more suitable, verified candidates.
  3. Interview: you meet the maid, at the office or at home, and ask about experience and expectations.
  4. Trial: she works for a short trial so both sides can judge the fit.
  5. Agreement: duties, salary, weekly off, leave and notice are written down and signed.
  6. Start and follow-up: the agency checks in during the first weeks to catch small issues early.
  7. Replacement if needed: if the placement does not work, a replacement is arranged under the agreed policy.

Your Responsibilities as an Employer

An agency can find and verify the right person, but a placement succeeds only if the household plays its part too. Agencies notice that the same families either keep maids for years or lose them every few months. The difference is usually how the job is handled:

  • Pay the agreed salary on the agreed date, every month.
  • Keep duties close to what was agreed, and discuss any change with fair adjustment.
  • Honour the weekly off and agreed leave.
  • For live-in maids, provide a private room, proper meals and real personal time.
  • Never keep a maid's CNIC or personal documents against her will.
  • Report problems to the agency early, before they grow into a sudden resignation.

Being a fair employer is not only the right thing to do; it also makes the agency's replacement guarantee easier to use if you ever need it, because the agency can see the problem was not caused by the household.

Different Placements an Agency Can Arrange

Most house maid agencies in Islamabad can arrange more than one type of placement. Knowing the options helps you ask for the right one:

  • Part-time maids who come for a few hours a day for cleaning, dishes or laundry.
  • Full-time day maids who work a fixed shift and return home in the evening.
  • Live-in maids who stay with the family, suitable for larger homes or families needing help early and late.
  • Maids with childcare experience who can support with children alongside housework, though a dedicated nanny is better for full-time childcare.
  • Maids with kitchen experience who help with cutting, cleaning up and simple meals, while a trained cook handles full cooking.

Demand for house maids comes from every part of the twin cities: apartments in E-11 and F-11, family houses in G-13 and I-8, larger homes in DHA, Bahria Town and Gulberg Greens, and established neighbourhoods across Rawalpindi such as Satellite Town, Saddar and Chaklala. Raising location early helps the agency suggest someone for whom the job is realistic in the long term.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if a house maid agency in Islamabad is genuine?

Look for a physical office, clear written terms, willingness to show verification documents and a defined replacement policy. Ask other families about their experience, and be cautious of agencies that only operate through a mobile number.

Can I meet the maid before she starts working?

Yes, and you should. A reliable agency arranges an interview and, ideally, a short trial so both you and the maid can judge whether the arrangement suits you.

What should a written agreement with a house maid include?

It should list duties, working hours, salary and payment date, weekly off, leave, notice period, and, for live-in maids, accommodation and meals.

Is police verification necessary for a house maid?

It is strongly recommended for live-in maids and for homes with children or elderly members. Ask the agency how it arranges verification and how long it takes.

What happens if the maid leaves after a few weeks?

That depends on the agency's replacement policy, so confirm the terms before you start. At ISB Domestic Staff, we arrange a replacement under our guarantee if a placement does not work out.
