House Help Services Islamabad

19 November 2026 · 8 min read · ISB Domestic Staff Team

Hiring help is only half the job. The other half is organising the work so your home actually stays clean, calm and well run. This practical guide to house help services in Islamabad goes beyond "find a maid." It shows you how to estimate how many hours of help your home needs, how to divide daily, weekly and monthly tasks, how Islamabad's seasons change the workload, and how to train new house help to your standards so you are not repeating instructions every day.

How Much House Help Does Your Home Really Need?

Families often over- or under-estimate the help they need. A rough guide, based on the size of the home and the household, can help you decide between part-time and full-time house help services in Islamabad.

Add extra time for pets, frequent guests, small children, elderly family members or homes with many bathrooms. When in doubt, start with a trial period and adjust hours after two or three weeks.

Daily, Weekly and Monthly: A Simple Task Plan

The biggest reason house help seems "not to be enough" is that tasks are not spread sensibly through the week. Splitting work by frequency keeps every corner of the house clean without overloading anyone.

Daily tasks

  • Sweeping and mopping main living areas and the kitchen
  • Washing dishes and wiping kitchen counters and the stove
  • Cleaning bathrooms used every day
  • Making beds and tidying bedrooms
  • Emptying rubbish bins
  • Quick dusting of high-use surfaces such as dining tables and TV units

Weekly tasks

  • Changing bed sheets and towels
  • Full dusting of furniture, shelves and decor
  • Cleaning mirrors, glass tables and window panes inside
  • Washing balconies, porches and car porches
  • Wiping kitchen cabinet fronts and appliances
  • Cleaning the fridge and checking for expired food

Monthly tasks

  • Cleaning fans, light fixtures and AC vents
  • Washing curtains or vacuuming them
  • Deep cleaning bathroom tiles and grout
  • Organising wardrobes, store rooms and kitchen shelves
  • Cleaning behind and under heavy furniture
  • Washing water tanks or arranging a tank cleaning service (every few months)

Families who use house help services in Islamabad for the first time often find that a written plan is the single biggest improvement they can make. It turns vague expectations into a clear routine that any capable worker can follow from the first week.

Write these lists down and give your house help a copy. A simple weekly rota, for example "Monday: bedrooms deep dust, Tuesday: kitchen cabinets", ensures nothing is forgotten.

Room-by-Room Cleaning Checklist

Kitchen

Counters, stove and sink wiped after every meal; floor mopped daily; exhaust hood and tiles behind the stove degreased weekly; spice racks and pantry organised monthly. In Pakistani kitchens, where cooking with oil is daily, grease builds up quickly, so weekly degreasing makes a visible difference.

Bathrooms

Toilets, sinks and floors cleaned daily in family bathrooms; guest bathrooms checked every few days; taps descaled weekly, especially in areas with hard water; drains cleared regularly to prevent blockages.

Bedrooms

Beds made and surfaces tidied daily; sheets changed weekly; mattresses turned and wardrobes aired monthly. Decide whether house help may open drawers and cupboards or only tidy visible surfaces.

Living and dining areas

Floors and coffee tables cleaned daily; sofas vacuumed and cushions fluffed weekly; carpets and rugs beaten or vacuumed regularly. Ask your provider whether your house help is comfortable using a vacuum cleaner or other appliances you own.

Outdoor areas

Porches, stairs and gates swept daily or every other day; lawns and plants handled by a gardener; rooftops cleared after storms and before monsoon.

Tip: Put a laminated checklist inside a kitchen cupboard door. It helps new house help learn your routine and allows anyone covering during leave to follow the same plan.

How Islamabad's Seasons Change the Workload

Islamabad's weather has a big effect on housework. Planning for seasonal changes helps your house help stay ahead instead of falling behind.

  • Spring (pollen season): the capital's paper mulberry pollen is notorious. Windows, sills, fans and curtains need more frequent cleaning, and families with allergies may want daily damp dusting rather than dry dusting, which spreads pollen.
  • Summer: heat and dust mean more frequent mopping, more laundry and regular cleaning of AC filters and coolers.
  • Monsoon: mud and damp require daily cleaning of entrances, checking for leaks and damp patches, and drying laundry indoors. Keep an eye on mould in bathrooms and store rooms.
  • Autumn: falling leaves increase outdoor sweeping and gutter clearing. It is also a good time for a full deep clean before winter.
  • Winter: heaters and gas fireplaces add soot and dust; windows stay closed, so airing rooms on sunny days matters. Smog days call for wiping surfaces more often.

Laundry and Ironing Routine

Laundry is one of the most time-consuming parts of housework, especially in families with school uniforms and office clothes. A clear routine helps:

  • Sort clothes into whites, colours and delicates, and show your house help which items must be hand-washed.
  • Fix washing days, for example uniforms on Monday and Thursday, and bedding on Monday.
  • Dry clothes safely, out of strong sunlight for coloured fabrics, and indoors during monsoon or smog.
  • Iron in batches two or three times a week, rather than every day.
  • Put clothes away in each person's wardrobe, or leave them folded in an agreed place if wardrobes are private.

Explain how your washing machine and iron work, and which fabrics need a lower heat setting, such as lawn and chiffon suits.

Supplies and Equipment Your House Help Needs

Even the best house help cannot do good work without proper tools. Keep a well-stocked cleaning cupboard:

  • Separate mops and buckets for floors and bathrooms
  • Microfibre cloths in different colours for kitchen, bathroom and general dusting
  • Floor cleaner, bathroom cleaner, glass cleaner and a mild degreaser
  • Long-handled brushes for fans and cobwebs, and a sturdy step ladder
  • Rubber gloves, and masks for dusty or chemical tasks
  • A vacuum cleaner if your home has carpets or many rugs

Explain which products are safe for marble, wooden furniture or delicate surfaces. Many floors in Islamabad homes are marble, which can be damaged by acidic cleaners.

Training New House Help to Your Standards

Every home is different, and even experienced workers need time to learn your preferences. A simple training approach in the first two weeks saves months of frustration:

  1. Show, don't just tell. Demonstrate how you like a bed made, towels folded or the sink cleaned.
  2. Start with priorities. Focus on the three or four things that matter most to you before adding details.
  3. Use the checklist. Walk through it together on day one and refer to it often.
  4. Give feedback the same day, calmly and specifically: "Please wipe under the kettle as well."
  5. Notice good work. Praise for a well-cleaned kitchen encourages consistency.
  6. Review after two weeks. Adjust the routine and hours based on what you have seen.

Part-Time vs Full-Time House Help: Getting the Balance Right

Part-time house help services in Islamabad suit smaller homes, people who work from home and families who prefer privacy. Full-time help suits larger homes, families with children and households that entertain frequently. Some families combine both: a full-time maid for daily work and a cleaning team once a month for deep cleaning. If you are unsure, discuss your routine with a provider and start with a trial.

Respect and Fair Treatment Keep Good House Help

A clean home depends on house help who want to stay. Fair, consistent treatment matters as much as clear instructions:

  • Pay salaries on the same date every month.
  • Keep working hours reasonable and allow proper breaks.
  • Provide drinking water, a meal or tea, and a place to rest.
  • Respect the weekly off and plan Eid leave in advance.
  • Never ask house help to do dangerous tasks, such as climbing onto roofs in the rain, without proper safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours of house help does a three-bedroom house need?

Many families find that four to six hours a day works well for a three-bedroom house, or full-time live-out help if there are young children, frequent guests or many bathrooms.

Can house help services in Islamabad include deep cleaning?

Yes. Your regular house help can handle weekly and monthly tasks, and for very large jobs such as moving house, a one-time deep cleaning team can be arranged.

What cleaning products should I avoid on marble floors?

Avoid acidic cleaners such as vinegar or lemon-based products, which can etch marble. Use a pH-neutral floor cleaner and clean spills quickly.

How do I train new house help to clean the way I like?

Demonstrate key tasks, give a written checklist, offer calm feedback the same day and review the routine after two weeks. Most workers adapt quickly with clear guidance.
