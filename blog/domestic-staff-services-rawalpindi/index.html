Domestic Staff

Domestic Staff Services Rawalpindi

18 November 2026 · 8 min read · ISB Domestic Staff Team

Most people do not search for domestic staff services in Rawalpindi because they woke up wanting a maid. They search because something in life has changed: a new baby, a parent's surgery, a wedding in the family, a move to Bahria Town or a second job that leaves no time for the house. This guide starts from those real situations and explains which domestic staff service fits each one, how long you might need it, and what to ask for so the help you get actually solves the problem.

Start With the Situation, Not the Job Title

Families often ask for the wrong service because they name a job title before thinking about the need. Someone who asks for "a maid" after a hip operation in the family may actually need a patient attendant. A couple who asks for "a nanny" for a toddler may really need a babysitter for four afternoon hours. Starting with the situation helps a provider recommend the right domestic staff services in Rawalpindi from the beginning.

Below are the most common situations Rawalpindi families face, with the service that usually fits best, how long it is typically needed and the questions worth asking before anyone starts work in your home.

Situation 1: A New Baby Has Arrived

The first months after a birth are exhausting, especially for families where both grandparents cannot stay to help.

  • Service that fits: a newborn nanny, either live-in or for long day shifts, plus a part-time maid for laundry and cleaning.
  • Typical duration: often three to six months, then a switch to a regular babysitter.
  • What to ask for: experience with infants, bottle sterilisation, bathing, burping and settling routines, and safe sleep habits.
  • Watch out for: asking the nanny to also cook and clean. The baby and mother should be her focus.

Situation 2: A Parent Is Coming Home From Hospital

After surgery, a stroke or a long illness, the first weeks at home are the most demanding. Families in Rawalpindi often bring patients home from CMH, Holy Family Hospital, Benazir Bhutto Hospital or private hospitals and suddenly realise the level of care needed.

  • Service that fits: a trained patient attendant; for patients needing night help, two attendants on day and night shifts.
  • Typical duration: from a few weeks for recovery to long-term care for chronic conditions.
  • What to ask for: experience with bedridden or post-operative patients, safe lifting, bed-sore prevention, catheter or diaper care, and feeding support.
  • Important: attendants support daily living; injections and medical procedures need a qualified nurse.

Situation 3: Both Partners Have Started Working Full Time

When a second income arrives, household time disappears. Dinner, laundry and school runs suddenly compete with office hours in Islamabad.

  • Service that fits: a live-out maid for cleaning and laundry, a cook for evening meals, and a driver if school and office timings clash.
  • Cost-saving option: a cook who also prepares the next day's lunch boxes, or a maid who comes on alternate days for a smaller home.
  • What to ask for: punctuality and reliability above everything else, because working parents depend on fixed routines.

Situation 4: A Wedding or Big Event at Home

Wedding season in Rawalpindi brings dholkis, mehndis and dinners at home, sometimes for weeks.

  • Service that fits: temporary event help, such as an extra cook or kitchen helper, cleaners before and after events, and someone to manage the gate and parking.
  • Typical duration: a few days to two weeks.
  • What to ask for: experience cooking in large quantities and working long, flexible hours.
  • Tip: book early. Demand for temporary help peaks during the main wedding months.

Situation 5: Moving to a Bigger House

Families moving from a flat or an older house in Saddar to a larger home in Bahria Town, DHA or Gulraiz are often surprised by how much more upkeep a bigger house needs.

  • Service that fits: a full-time maid or housekeeper, a gardener (mali) for the lawn, and possibly a watchman (chowkidar).
  • What to ask for: experience with larger homes, marble or tiled floors, and coordinating with other staff.
  • Before the move: a one-time deep cleaning team can prepare the new house so your regular staff start fresh.

Situation 6: An Elderly Parent Lives Alone

Many sons and daughters living in Islamabad or abroad worry about parents who still live alone in Satellite Town, Westridge or Chaklala.

  • Service that fits: a live-in caregiver or companion attendant, or a full-time maid who can also cook simple meals and give medicine reminders.
  • What to ask for: patience, honesty, and the ability to share regular updates with family members by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Extra safety: police verification is strongly recommended when the worker will be alone with an elderly person.

Situation 7: Running a Shop or Office

Small businesses in Raja Bazaar, Saddar, Committee Chowk and Murree Road need dependable support staff too.

  • Service that fits: an office boy for tea, errands, photocopies and deliveries, and a cleaner for daily upkeep.
  • What to ask for: trustworthiness with cash and documents, punctuality and a respectful manner with customers.

Situation 8: Your Regular Staff Are Going Home for Eid

Almost every household in Rawalpindi faces this twice a year. When regular staff travel to their villages, the house can come to a standstill.

  • Service that fits: short-term cover for the most essential role, usually cooking, childcare or patient care.
  • What to ask for: a clear start and end date, and a short handover from your regular worker before they leave.
  • Tip: request cover two to three weeks ahead, as agencies receive many requests just before Eid.

Situation 9: Family Visiting From Abroad

Rawalpindi has a large overseas community, and many families return from the Gulf, the UK or Europe for a few weeks or months each year. Their homes may have been closed for a long time, and relatives often gather for the whole visit.

  • Service that fits: a deep cleaning team before arrival, then a temporary maid and cook for the length of the stay, and sometimes a driver for family visits and shopping.
  • Typical duration: two weeks to three months.
  • What to ask for: flexibility with irregular hours, comfort with large family gatherings, and willingness to work for a fixed period.
  • Tip: overseas families can arrange domestic staff services in Rawalpindi before they fly, so help is ready the day they land. A relative or the provider can let staff in and supervise the first clean.

Families who visit every year often ask for the same trusted workers each time. A provider that keeps good records can usually reconnect you with staff who already know your home.

Remember: Your needs will change over time. The right service today may not be the right one next year, so review your arrangements whenever your family situation changes.

How to Brief a Provider for Any Situation

Whatever your situation, giving clear information helps a provider of domestic staff services in Rawalpindi recommend the right person quickly. Share:

  1. What has changed in your household and why you need help now.
  2. Your exact location, for example Satellite Town Block C or Bahria Town Phase 7.
  3. The hours and days you need help, and whether live-in is possible.
  4. Who the worker will mainly support: a baby, a patient, the whole family or a business.
  5. Any non-negotiables, such as a female worker for an elderly mother or a driver comfortable with long trips.
  6. Your expected duration: a few weeks, a few months or long term.
  7. Your budget range, so the provider can suggest realistic options.

Keeping Costs Sensible

Domestic staff services in Rawalpindi are priced according to the role, and costs depend on experience, hours and whether the worker lives in. A few practical ways to manage the budget without compromising safety:

  • Use part-time help for tasks that do not need a full day, such as cleaning a small flat.
  • Combine compatible roles, like cooking and kitchen cleaning, but avoid combining childcare or patient care with heavy housework.
  • Use temporary services for short-term needs instead of hiring permanent staff you will not need later.
  • Ask your provider for current market rates rather than relying on outdated figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which domestic staff service do I need after a family member's surgery?

Usually a trained patient attendant, and for patients who need help at night, two attendants working day and night shifts. If injections or medical procedures are required, you will also need a qualified nurse.

Can I hire domestic staff in Rawalpindi for just a few weeks?

Yes. Temporary services are common for weddings, Eid cover, newborn care and recovery after illness. Agree on clear start and end dates when you book.

Is it better to hire a nanny or a babysitter?

A nanny usually works full time and handles all aspects of a child's day, while a babysitter covers shorter periods such as afternoons or evenings. Newborns and very young children often need a full-time nanny.

Do you provide event helpers for weddings at home?

Yes. ISB Domestic Staff can arrange extra cooks, kitchen helpers and cleaners for weddings and family events. Book early during the main wedding season.
