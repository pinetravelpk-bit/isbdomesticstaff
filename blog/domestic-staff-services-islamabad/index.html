Domestic Staff Services Islamabad

17 November 2026 · 8 min read · ISB Domestic Staff Team

The phrase domestic staff services in Islamabad covers far more than a cleaning maid. Today, families and offices in the capital can arrange trained help for almost every part of daily life, from cooking and childcare to patient care, driving and security. This guide walks through each service one by one: what is normally included, what is not, who it suits, and what to look for in the person you hire. Use it as a reference before you call an agency, so you know exactly which service to ask for.

Maid Services

Usually included: sweeping and mopping, dusting, cleaning bathrooms and kitchen surfaces, washing dishes, laundry and ironing, changing bed sheets, and taking out rubbish.

Usually not included: cooking full meals, looking after infants, heavy outdoor work or deep cleaning of carpets and sofas, unless agreed separately.

Best for: almost every household, from a two-bedroom flat in G-11 to a family house in F-8. Part-time maids suit smaller homes and working couples; full-time or live-in maids suit larger houses and families with children.

What to look for: attention to detail, honesty, respect for privacy, and experience with the type of home you have, for example marble floors or a large number of bathrooms.

Housekeeping Services

A housekeeper is a step above a maid. Instead of just doing tasks, a housekeeper manages the house.

  • Planning daily and weekly cleaning schedules
  • Keeping track of groceries, cleaning supplies and household inventory
  • Supervising maids, cleaners or kitchen helpers
  • Preparing guest rooms and organising wardrobes and storage
  • Handling deliveries and coordinating repair visits

Best for: large homes in areas such as F-6, F-7, DHA and Bahria Town, busy executives and families who host often. A capable housekeeper can save the head of the house many hours every week.

Cook Services

Cook services in Islamabad range from a cook who prepares one evening meal to a full-time family cook handling breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Usually included: menu planning with the family, cutting and preparation, cooking Pakistani dishes, basic continental food if agreed, keeping the kitchen clean, and preparing grocery lists.

Worth discussing: special diets (diabetic, low-oil, low-salt), baking, cooking for parties and guests, and whether the cook also does the grocery shopping.

What to look for: hygiene habits, the ability to cook your family's favourite dishes the way you like them, and flexibility for guests. A short cooking trial is the best test.

Driver Services

Usually included: school and office drops, errands, hospital visits, family outings, and basic car care such as washing, checking oil and tyre pressure, and arranging servicing.

Worth discussing: working hours and late evenings, out-of-city trips to Murree, Lahore or the northern areas, overtime arrangements and whether the driver will also handle errands like bill payments.

What to look for: a valid driving licence for the vehicle type, a clean record, knowledge of Islamabad and Rawalpindi routes, calm driving habits and punctuality. For families with children, patience and responsibility matter as much as driving skill.

Nanny and Babysitting Services

Childcare is one of the most sensitive domestic staff services in Islamabad, and it should be treated that way.

  • Newborn nannies help with feeding routines, bathing, sterilising bottles, night duty and supporting the mother after delivery.
  • Toddler and child nannies handle meals, play, naps, hygiene and outings.
  • Babysitters provide part-time or occasional supervision, for example after school or during evenings out.

What to look for: genuine warmth with children, real childcare experience, cleanliness, and calmness under pressure. Ask how they would respond to a fever, a fall or a choking incident. A trial day where you watch the nanny with your child is strongly recommended.

Important: Keep childcare and housework as separate roles wherever possible. A nanny who is also expected to clean the whole house cannot give your children full attention.

Patient and Elderly Care Services

Many families in Islamabad and Rawalpindi now care for ageing parents at home. Home patient care services provide trained attendants who support daily living.

Usually included: help with bathing, dressing and toileting; turning bedridden patients to prevent bed sores; assistance with walking and wheelchairs; medicine reminders; feeding; companionship; and accompanying the patient to appointments.

Not the same as nursing: attendants do not normally give injections, manage IV lines or make medical decisions. If your patient needs clinical procedures, ask about a qualified home nurse in addition to an attendant.

Shift options: day shift, night shift or a live-in attendant. For patients needing round-the-clock care, two attendants on rotating shifts are safer than one person on duty 24 hours.

Watchman and Security Services

A chowkidar or watchman controls who enters your property. Duties usually include gate duty, checking visitors and deliveries, keeping a basic log, night rounds, and reporting anything unusual.

What to look for: alertness, reliability, a verified background and, for night duty, a healthy routine so they can stay awake. Police verification is particularly important for this role.

Office Boy and Cleaning Services

Domestic staffing does not end at home. Offices in Blue Area, I-8, G-8 and Saddar often need dependable support staff for tea and refreshments, keeping meeting rooms tidy, photocopying and filing, delivering documents and daily cleaning. A trustworthy office boy becomes part of the team and handles small tasks that keep an office running smoothly.

Gardener (Mali) Services

Islamabad's greenery is part of its charm, and many homes have lawns and plants that need regular care. A mali handles mowing, watering, trimming hedges, planting seasonal flowers, composting and pest control for plants. Some families hire a full-time gardener; others prefer visits on alternate days or once a week.

Service Formats: Full-Time, Part-Time, Live-In and Temporary

Each of the services above can usually be arranged in several formats:

  1. Full-time live-in: the worker lives at your home, ideal for nannies, patient attendants and large households.
  2. Full-time live-out: fixed daily hours, with the worker returning home each evening.
  3. Part-time: a few hours a day or certain days a week, common for maids, cooks and gardeners.
  4. Temporary cover: short-term help during Eid, when your regular staff are on leave, or after a new baby or hospital discharge.
  5. Event help: extra cooks, helpers or cleaners for weddings, dinners and family gatherings.

Many households also combine formats, for example a live-in nanny with a part-time maid, or a full-time driver with a gardener who visits twice a week. Mixing formats lets you cover every essential task without paying for full-time hours where they are not needed, and it gives each worker a clear, manageable role.

Choosing the right format is as important as choosing the right person. Discuss your schedule openly so the agency can suggest the most practical arrangement.

Questions to Settle Before Booking Any Service

Whichever domestic staff service you choose, a few practical points should be clear before the worker starts. Agreeing on them early prevents most misunderstandings later:

  • Exact duties: list what is included and, just as importantly, what is not.
  • Timings: start and finish times, break times and how often late evenings may be needed.
  • Weekly off and leave: which day off, how many days of annual leave and the arrangement for Eid.
  • Salary and payment date: the monthly amount, the date it is paid, and any overtime or travel allowance.
  • Meals and accommodation: for live-in staff, where they will stay and what meals are provided.
  • Notice period: how much notice either side gives before ending the arrangement.
  • Trial period: how long the trial lasts and what happens if it does not work out.

Preparing Your Home for New Staff

A little preparation helps new staff settle in quickly and do their best work from the first day:

  1. Keep cleaning supplies, uniforms (if provided) and tools ready and in one place.
  2. For live-in staff, prepare a clean room with a bed, fan or heater, and access to a bathroom.
  3. Write down important routines, such as school timings, medicine schedules or meal preferences.
  4. Lock away valuables and important documents. This protects both the family and the worker from misunderstandings.
  5. Save emergency contacts on a sheet in the kitchen: family members, nearby hospital, and the agency's number.

Families who prepare in this way usually find that new staff become confident and independent within the first two weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which domestic staff services are most in demand in Islamabad?

Maids, cooks and drivers are the most common requests, followed closely by nannies and patient attendants as more families have working parents and elderly relatives at home.

Can I hire domestic staff for only a few hours a day?

Yes. Part-time maid, cook and gardener services are widely available in Islamabad, usually for a fixed number of hours on agreed days each week.

Do patient attendants give medicines and injections?

Attendants can remind patients to take medicines and help them take oral medication, but injections, drips and other clinical tasks should be handled by a qualified nurse.

Can I get temporary staff during Eid or my regular worker's leave?

Many agencies can arrange short-term cover. Request it a few weeks in advance, especially before Eid, when demand is very high.

Are domestic staff services also available for offices?

Yes. ISB Domestic Staff provides office boys, office helpers and cleaning staff for offices across Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
