Maids & Housekeepers

Hire Maid in Islamabad

21 November 2026 · 8 min read · ISB Domestic Staff Team

A good maid can transform daily life: floors that stay clean, laundry that never piles up, and evenings that belong to your family instead of to housework. But when families decide to hire a maid in Islamabad, they often discover how many choices are involved. Part-time or full-time? Live-in or live-out? Cleaning only, or cleaning plus some cooking? This guide focuses entirely on maids: the types of arrangements available, what a maid's job usually includes, which skills to check, how to run a practical trial and how to set your new maid up for success from the first day.

Choosing the right arrangement first makes everything else easier, from the salary you offer to the candidates an agency sends you.

What a Maid Usually Does, and What She Usually Doesn't

Typically included

  • Sweeping, mopping and dusting
  • Cleaning bathrooms and kitchen surfaces
  • Washing dishes
  • Laundry, ironing and folding
  • Making beds and changing sheets
  • Taking out rubbish and keeping rooms tidy

Usually not included unless agreed

  • Cooking full meals for the family
  • Full-time childcare or caring for infants
  • Personal care for elderly or sick family members
  • Heavy outdoor work, gardening or washing cars
  • Deep cleaning of carpets, sofas or water tanks

If you need any of the second list, say so clearly when you hire. Adding them later without discussion is one of the most common reasons maids leave.

Skills to Check When You Hire a Maid in Islamabad

Not all maids have the same experience. Depending on your home, look for:

  • Floor care: many Islamabad homes have marble or tiled floors that need the right cleaning products and techniques.
  • Laundry skills: sorting colours, handling delicate fabrics such as lawn and chiffon, and using your washing machine correctly.
  • Ironing: pressing shalwar kameez, uniforms and office shirts neatly.
  • Organisation: keeping wardrobes, kitchen cupboards and store rooms in order.
  • Appliance use: vacuum cleaners, washing machines and dryers.
  • Hygiene: separate cloths for kitchen and bathroom, handwashing, careful handling of food areas.
  • Reliability and honesty: confirmed through references from previous employers.

Where to Find a Maid

Families in Islamabad typically find maids through referrals from neighbours, informal brokers or a verified agency. Referrals can work if the person recommending has employed the maid directly for a long time. For live-in maids, or any maid who will be alone in your home, a verified agency adds identity checks, reference calls, written terms and a replacement guarantee. Interview Questions for Maids

  1. How many homes have you worked in, and for how long?
  2. What did a normal day look like in your last job?
  3. How do you clean marble floors without damaging them?
  4. How do you wash and iron delicate clothes?
  5. Have you used a washing machine and vacuum cleaner?
  6. Are you comfortable with pets? (if relevant)
  7. How will you travel to our house each day? (for live-out roles)
  8. Is there any task you prefer not to do?

Listen for practical, specific answers. A maid who explains how she organised a weekly cleaning routine in her last job is usually more experienced than one who simply says she "can do everything."

Running a Practical Trial Day

A paid trial day is the best way to see a maid's real skills. Plan tasks that reflect the actual job:

  • Clean one bedroom and one bathroom thoroughly.
  • Mop the lounge and kitchen floors.
  • Wash a load of laundry and iron a few items.
  • Wash the dishes after lunch.

Watch for thoroughness, care with your belongings, hygiene and attitude. Notice whether she asks questions when unsure. At the end of the day, pay the agreed amount and ask how she found the work.

Tip: Show the candidate your cleaning supplies and appliances before the trial starts. Judging skill is only fair if she knows what tools she has.

Sample Daily Schedules

Part-time maid in a two-bedroom apartment (3 hours)

  • Dishes and kitchen surfaces
  • Sweeping and mopping all rooms
  • Cleaning bathrooms
  • Laundry on alternate days

Full-time maid in a four-bedroom house

  • Morning: breakfast dishes, kitchen, making beds, tidying bedrooms
  • Late morning: sweeping and mopping, bathrooms
  • Afternoon: laundry, ironing, weekly task from the rota
  • Evening (before leaving): dinner dishes or final tidy-up as agreed

Red Flags When You Hire a Maid in Islamabad

  • No original CNIC, or reluctance to share an address.
  • "References" who cannot describe her duties or the house she worked in.
  • Unwillingness to do a paid trial day.
  • Requests for a large advance before starting.
  • Vague answers about why she left previous jobs.
  • A broker who insists on receiving her salary on her behalf.

One warning sign is not always disqualifying, but several together should make you pause and ask for a different candidate.

Maids for Special Household Situations

Homes with pets

Some maids are uncomfortable around dogs or cats. Mention pets at the start, and ask whether she is willing to clean up after them or feed them.

Family members with allergies or asthma

Ask for damp dusting rather than dry dusting, fragrance-free cleaning products and extra attention during Islamabad's spring pollen season.

A new baby in the house

Hygiene becomes even more important. Make sure your maid understands sterilising areas, washing baby clothes separately and keeping floors spotless. If you also need childcare, hire a nanny rather than adding it to the maid's duties.

Elderly family members at home

A patient, respectful maid who keeps floors dry and pathways clear helps prevent falls. Personal care such as bathing should be handled by a trained attendant.

When Two Part-Time Maids Work Better Than One

For some homes, splitting the work between two part-time maids is more effective than one full-time worker. For example, one maid could handle daily cleaning in the morning while another comes three afternoons a week for laundry and ironing. This gives you backup when one is absent, keeps each worker's load reasonable and can suit families who prefer fewer hours of help in the house at any one time. If you choose this route, give each maid a clear, separate list of duties so nothing is missed or duplicated.

What Affects a Maid's Salary

Maid salaries in Islamabad vary widely. Key factors include:

  • Part-time or full-time hours
  • Live-in or live-out arrangement
  • Size of the house and number of family members
  • Extra duties such as cooking or babysitting
  • Years of experience and quality of references
  • Location and commute distance

Ask your agency for current market rates for the exact arrangement you need. Offering a fair salary from the start attracts more experienced maids and reduces turnover.

Safety and Dignity for Female Maids

Most maids in Islamabad are women, and many work alone in homes. Families can make sure the workplace is safe and respectful by:

  • Setting clear rules for how all household members and guests behave towards staff.
  • Providing a private, lockable room for live-in maids.
  • Allowing her to keep her phone and contact her family.
  • Making sure she knows she can raise concerns with you or the agency at any time.
  • Never asking her to work alone with male guests in situations that make her uncomfortable.

A maid who feels safe is far more likely to stay and do excellent work.

Maids in Gated Societies and Apartment Buildings

If you live in DHA, Bahria Town, Bahria Enclave or a managed apartment building in E-11 or G-11, check the entry rules before your maid's first day. Many societies ask residents to register domestic workers, provide a CNIC copy or collect an entry card. Some buildings set times when staff may enter or use particular lifts and staircases. Sorting this out in advance avoids an awkward first morning at the gate, and when you hire a maid in Islamabad through an agency, they can usually prepare the documents your society needs.

The First Week With Your New Maid

  1. Walk through the house and explain which areas are private.
  2. Show where supplies are kept and how appliances work.
  3. Share a written daily and weekly task list.
  4. Demonstrate your preferences, such as how you like beds made or clothes folded.
  5. Give calm, specific feedback the same day.
  6. End the week with a short conversation about how things are going.

Keeping a Good Maid for the Long Term

  • Pay on the same date every month.
  • Keep duties close to what was agreed, and review pay if duties increase.
  • Respect the weekly off and plan Eid leave in advance.
  • Provide drinking water, tea or a meal, and a place to rest.
  • Thank her for good work. Recognition matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I hire a maid in Islamabad?

Through a verified agency, suitable maids can often be shortlisted within a few days, with interviews and a trial day arranged soon after.

Should I hire a part-time or full-time maid?

Part-time maids suit apartments and smaller homes. Full-time or live-in maids suit larger houses, families with children and households that entertain often.

Can a maid also cook?

Some maids can prepare simple meals, but cooking should be agreed in advance and reflected in the salary. For regular family meals, a dedicated cook is often better.

What should I check before hiring a maid?

Check her original CNIC, confirm her address, speak to previous employers and run a paid trial day. For live-in maids, police verification is also recommended.
