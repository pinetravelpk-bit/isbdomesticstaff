A part-time maid who comes for two hours can keep a small flat tidy. But when the house is larger, the children are young or both parents work long hours, two hours is rarely enough. That is when families start looking for a full time maid in Islamabad: someone who arrives in the morning, works through the day and goes home in the evening. This guide explains what full-time really means, how it compares with part-time and live-in help, what a realistic daily schedule looks like, and how to set up the arrangement so it lasts.

What "Full-Time" Usually Means

In most Islamabad and Rawalpindi households, a full-time maid is a day-shift worker. She typically:

  • Arrives in the morning and leaves in the late afternoon or early evening
  • Works six days a week with one agreed day off
  • Handles the main housekeeping of the home rather than a single task
  • Lives in her own home and travels to work each day

The exact hours vary from family to family, so always agree on them clearly. "Full-time" should mean a defined shift, not "available whenever needed."

Full-Time, Part-Time or Live-In: Which Fits Your Home?

Choosing the wrong arrangement is one of the most common reasons placements fail. Here is how the three options compare in practice.

Part-time maid

Comes for a few hours, once or twice a day. Suits small flats, couples or families who only need help with cleaning and dishes. It is flexible, but there is no one at home for most of the day, and larger tasks such as laundry and ironing often pile up.

Full-time maid

Works a full day shift. Suits medium and large homes, families with children, and households where both parents work. She can handle cleaning, laundry, ironing, kitchen help and general upkeep in one day, and the family keeps its evenings private.

Live-in maid

Stays with the family. Suits households needing help early in the morning and late at night, or homes in outer housing societies where daily travel is difficult. It requires a private room and careful attention to off-duty time.

A simple test: If your home needs more than four or five hours of work most days, but you value privacy in the evenings, a full time maid in Islamabad is usually the right fit.

Signs You Have Outgrown Part-Time Help

Many families start with part-time help and move to full-time as their needs grow. Common signs include:

  • Laundry and ironing are always behind, even though the floors are clean.
  • You spend your weekends catching up on housework.
  • The kitchen is a mess again by the afternoon, after the maid has left.
  • A new baby, an elderly parent or a bigger house has added work.
  • You find yourself hiring a second part-time worker for the tasks the first cannot fit in.

If several of these sound familiar, a single full-time maid often costs less in stress, and sometimes in money, than juggling several part-time arrangements.

A Realistic Daily Schedule

Every home is different, but a typical day for a full time maid in Islamabad might look something like this:

  • Morning arrival: clear breakfast dishes, wipe the kitchen, start the first laundry load.
  • Mid-morning: sweep and mop floors, clean bathrooms, make beds and tidy bedrooms.
  • Late morning: dusting, wiping surfaces, emptying bins, hanging laundry to dry.
  • Lunch break: a proper meal and rest, agreed in advance.
  • Early afternoon: lunch dishes, kitchen help such as cutting vegetables, folding dry clothes.
  • Afternoon: ironing, one weekly task such as cleaning windows, fans or cupboards.
  • Before leaving: a final tidy of living areas and kitchen so the evening starts clean.

Spreading weekly tasks across the week, such as fans on Monday and cupboards on Wednesday, keeps the workload steady instead of overwhelming.

Matching Workload to Home Size

A fair workload depends on the size of your home and the number of people in it. A full-time maid in a two-bedroom flat in E-11 can usually manage everything with time to spare. In a large house in DHA, Bahria Town or F-7 with several bedrooms, a lawn and a big family, one maid may only manage cleaning and laundry, and cooking or childcare may need another person.

Be honest with yourself and the agency about how much work there is. Overloading one full-time maid is a fast way to lose her.

Hours, Breaks and Overtime

Clear time boundaries are what make full-time arrangements work.

  • Fixed start and finish times: write them down and respect them.
  • A real lunch break plus short rest breaks during the day.
  • One weekly day off, agreed in advance and honoured.
  • Extra hours by agreement only: for a dinner party or family function, ask in advance and pay fairly for the additional time.
  • Winter timings: consider letting her leave earlier when it gets dark sooner, especially if she travels by public transport.

When finishing time keeps sliding later without discussion, the arrangement quietly turns into an unpaid live-in job, and resentment follows.

Commuting and Location

Because a full-time maid travels to work every day, location matters more than many families expect. A maid living in Rawalpindi may reach Saddar, Satellite Town or Chaklala easily, but a daily trip to Islamabad's outer sectors or societies along the GT Road or Kashmir Highway can take a long time and cost a significant part of her income.

  • Tell the agency your exact area so it can suggest someone who lives within reasonable reach.
  • Discuss how she will travel, whether by Metro bus, van or on foot.
  • Some families contribute towards transport, especially for longer journeys.
  • Agree on what happens on days of heavy rain, road closures or strikes.

A maid with a manageable commute is far more likely to be punctual and stay long term.

Salary and Budgeting

Salaries for a full-time maid in Islamabad depend on experience, the duties involved, the size of your home and your location. Rather than relying on what a neighbour pays, discuss the role openly with the agency and the candidate. When budgeting, also think about:

  • Meals or tea during working hours
  • Any agreed transport contribution
  • An Eid bonus, which is customary
  • A periodic increase for continued good service

Whatever you agree, pay on a fixed date every month. Late payment damages trust faster than almost anything else.

Putting the Arrangement in Writing

A short written agreement protects both sides. It should cover:

  1. Daily and weekly duties
  2. Working days, start and finish times, and breaks
  3. Monthly salary and payment date
  4. Weekly day off, annual leave and Eid holidays
  5. How extra hours are requested and paid
  6. Notice period on both sides

Agencies usually help prepare this. If you hire on your own, even a simple one-page document signed by both parties is better than nothing.

Checks Before She Starts

A full-time maid will spend most of the day in your home, often while you are out. Verification matters:

  • Original CNIC seen and copied
  • Permanent address and a family contact confirmed
  • At least one previous employer contacted
  • Police verification where children or elderly members are at home alone with her
  • A short paid trial before final agreement

Making It Work for Working Parents

For households where both parents leave for work, a full time maid in Islamabad often becomes the person who keeps the home steady during the day. A few practical habits help:

  • Leave a daily note or WhatsApp message with priorities, such as guests arriving or school uniforms to iron.
  • Agree on house keys and what happens if she arrives before anyone is home.
  • Set rules about visitors and deliveries while you are out.
  • Keep emergency numbers visible: family members, a neighbour and the agency.
  • Overlap occasionally: try to be home for the first or last hour sometimes, so problems are discussed in person.

Keeping a Good Full-Time Maid

Finding a reliable full-time maid takes effort, so retention is worth planning for. The families who keep the same maid for years usually:

  • Keep duties close to what was agreed and discuss any change
  • Let her leave on time
  • Give praise as well as correction
  • Allow leave for genuine family needs
  • Review salary fairly over time

When One Full-Time Maid Is Not Enough

Some households need more than one person, and recognising this early avoids burning out a good worker. Consider adding staff if:

  • You need full cooking every day for a large family, in addition to housekeeping. A cook alongside a maid is usually more effective.
  • There is a newborn or toddler who needs constant attention. A nanny allows the maid to keep the house running.
  • An elderly parent needs regular personal care, which is better handled by a trained attendant.
  • The house has extensive outdoor areas, such as lawns and driveways, that need a gardener or helper.

Splitting the work between two or three people with clear roles is often more stable than expecting one full-time maid to do everything. A good agency can help you plan the right combination of staff for your home and budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the usual working hours for a full time maid in Islamabad?

Most work a daytime shift from morning to late afternoon or early evening, six days a week. The exact hours should be agreed and written down before she starts.

Is a full-time maid better than a live-in maid?

It depends on your needs. A full-time maid gives you private evenings and needs no accommodation, while a live-in maid suits families who need help early in the morning or late at night.

Can a full-time maid also cook?

Many can help in the kitchen with cutting, cleaning and simple dishes. For full family cooking, a dedicated cook is usually the better choice.

Should I pay extra if she stays late?

Yes. Extra hours should be requested in advance and paid fairly, so that occasional late days do not turn into unpaid overtime.
